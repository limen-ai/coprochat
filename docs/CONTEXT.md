# CoproChat — Context & Origin

## What It Is
Translator from normal human language to corporate VP-speak bullshit.

**Domain:** copro.chat  
**Product name:** CoproChat

## The Name (it's a pun)
- **Surface:** sounds like "corpo" (corporate)
- **Hidden:** "copro" from coprophagia (eating feces)
- **Combined meaning:** corporate bullshit = literal shit-eating

The 💩 emoji in the UI isn't just decoration. It's the joke.

## Origin Story

### The VP Message That Started It
Boris's actual VP sent this gem:

> FYI folks. We're going to be shutting down various non-core features of the Command Center prototype (e.g. Learning Centre / Leaderboards) to better focus everyone's efforts on the critical elements that will directly support our number one priority which is to better enable MB's and CSM's by unifying our various data assets/reports/dashboards into a single portal. We'll look to re-introduce ancillary capabilities in the future once we've nailed the base product! The more everyone contributes, the better the end product will be, so please continue to keep core feature ideas & feedback flowing!! 🚀

Translation: "We're killing features. Deal with it."

### The $280k IT Director Article
Pseudonymous IT Director explaining how corporate success is about perception, not performance. Key techniques that inspired the translator:

**Strategic Incompetence** — making things you don't want to do sound so complicated that people stop asking:
- "We'd need to rebuild the entire authentication infrastructure" = I don't want to do this
- "That would require significant vendor coordination" = I don't feel like calling anyone
- "Let me put together a feasibility analysis" = I'm going to wait until you forget

**The language patterns:**
- Unnecessary openers: "FYI folks", "Quick update", "Excited to share"
- Padding: "better enable", "strategic alignment", "cross-functional synergy"
- Negative → Positive spin: "cutting features" → "strategically reallocating resources"
- Forced enthusiasm + meaningless CTAs: "Keep the ideas flowing! 🚀"
- Deflection: "requires further analysis", "circle back in Q3"

**The insight:** "Being good at technology is the least important part of being successful in corporate IT... perception matters more than performance."

## Why It's Funny
1. Everyone recognizes this language from their own jobs
2. The contrast between what you mean and what comes out
3. Forced positivity masking bad news or laziness
4. Passive-aggressive undertones hidden in "professionalism"
5. Making the implicit explicit — we all know this is bullshit, the app just generates it on demand

## Target Audience
- Anyone who's sat in corporate meetings
- People who need to write emails they don't mean
- Office workers who want to laugh at the absurdity
- Viral sharing potential: "look what it did to my honest thought"
